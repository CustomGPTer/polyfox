How it works

A little every day beats a lot now and then. PolyFox is built around a few minutes a day.

  1. Pick one language

    Choose a course and a daily goal. If you already know some, the level check finds your starting unit.

  2. Do a lesson

    About four minutes: a few new words with pictures and sound, then sentences to build, hear, type and say.

  3. Let it come back

    What you learned returns in later lessons and in practice, more often when you find it hard and less when you know it.

Everything is in, and all of it is free

French, German, Portuguese, Spanish
European Portuguese and the Spanish of Spain. Each course is written for its own language, so the grammar is taught in the order that suits it.
A1 to C1 today

One more level to come

Lessons

Fifteen kinds of exercise
Pictures, matching pairs, word tiles, typing, listening, fill the gap, choose the form, spot the error, dialogues, reading and speaking.
Both directions
English into the language and the language into English. Lean it either way in Settings.
Old words keep coming back
Each lesson mixes in earlier words, chosen by how well you remember them, so they stick without a separate revision chore.
Tap any word

A guide for every unit
The grammar set out in plain English with examples, before or after the lessons.

Stories and exams

An exam for each level
Thirty questions drawn from the whole level, with no hints. Score 70% to open the next level, and retake it whenever you like.
Five ways to practise
Smart review, weak spots, recent mistakes, listening and speaking.

Installing and updating

PolyFox is not on Google Play, so your phone asks a couple of extra questions the first time.

  1. Download the app file

    Tap Download on your phone. The file is called PolyFox.apk and is about 158 MB, so wifi is best. If Chrome says the file might be harmful, choose Download anyway.

  2. Allow the install

    Open the file. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from your browser. Allow it, go back, and tap Install.

  3. Open PolyFox

    Type a name, pick a language and a daily goal, and start the first lesson.

To update, download the newest version and install it over the old one. Your progress, streak and settings are kept.

Questions

Is PolyFox really free?

Yes. There is no paid version, no subscription, no trial and no adverts, and nothing in the app is locked behind payment.

Is PolyFox on Google Play?

No. PolyFox is a personal project and is downloaded from this site as an Android app file. It needs Android 8 or later.

Does it need the internet?

No. The app does not ask Android for internet access at all, so it works anywhere and cannot go online. Check for updates and More apps only open a page in your browser.

Why is the download so large?

The app file is about 158 MB because every word and sentence has its own recording, more than 24,000 clips across the four courses. Nothing is downloaded later.

How far do the courses go?

This version covers levels A1, A2, B1, B2 and C1 of the European framework: from hello to nuanced argument, irony, rhetoric and academic language. Level C2 is being written and arrives as an update.

Can I learn two languages at once?

One course is active at a time, because that is how languages stick. You can switch course whenever you like, and each one keeps its own place.

Which Portuguese and which Spanish?

European Portuguese, as spoken in Portugal, and the Spanish of Spain. Where Latin American usage differs in a way that matters, the unit guide says so.

Are the voices real people?

They are synthetic voices, made in advance and stored in the app. They are credited on the licences page.

Who wrote the courses?

The courses are written for PolyFox and have not yet been reviewed by native speakers. If something looks wrong, tap the flag after a question and send the list from Settings.

Does it listen to me?

Only in speaking exercises, and only after you tap record. The recording stays on the phone so you can play it back against the model voice, and the next one replaces it. PolyFox does not mark your pronunciation.

How do I update PolyFox?

Download the newest version from this site and install it over the one you have. Your progress is kept. The app cannot check for updates itself because it cannot go online.
