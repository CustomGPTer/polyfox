Release notes

Install a new version over the old one. Your progress, streak and settings are kept.

1.0.1 24 November 2026

Fixes from an independent review of 1.0.0. Install it over 1.0.0: your progress, streak and settings are kept.

  • Your answers are saved as you go, so none are lost if Android closes the app in the middle of a lesson.
  • Typing in conversations now works: the Send button no longer takes up the whole row.
  • Speaking is stricter about meaning: a different number (9 and 99), a negative added or left out, or a different key word no longer passes. Clear speech passes as often as before.
  • Speaking practice shows and plays the man's or woman's form you chose, with new recordings for the 71 lines that change.
  • If the phone cannot check a recording, it says so and lets you try again or mark it yourself.
  • Backups are checked before restoring: a damaged or edited file is refused and your progress is left as it was. Restoring also brings back the daily reminder.
  • If the phone is too full to save your progress, the app tells you and tries again.
  • Progress shows your real level, from A1 Beginner to C2 Proficient. The course counts as finished once the C2 exam is passed.
  • Recent mistakes clear once you get them right, and Smart review's count matches what it offers.
  • Looking up a word, marking yourself, or one wrong pair in matching brings the word back a little sooner.
  • Time with the app in the background no longer counts towards your daily goal.
  • Screen readers name every picture choice.
  • The speech model is loaded once and its memory is freed when you close the app.

Download 1.0.1

1.0.0 23 November 2026

PolyFox is now PolyFox French: one language, made as good as it can be. German, Portuguese and Spanish will follow as their own apps.

  • French only, with a new icon: the fox in front of the French flag. It installs over 0.7.1 and keeps your French progress. Progress in the other courses is kept in the app's file for the language apps that follow.
  • Speaking is now checked on the phone. A speech model inside the app shows which words it understood, in green and gold. Nothing is sent anywhere, and you can still mark yourself.
  • Twelve conversations, from the café to a job interview. Say or type your part, or tap Help to see what you can say.
  • Every correct answer counts: over 690 other wordings are accepted, such as tu or vous, inversion, est-ce que and numbers as digits.
  • Choose whether lessons teach the man's or the woman's form of lines like "je suis fatigué(e)". Both are always accepted.
  • A wrong answer now shows which words were wrong and why: gender, elision, negatives, agreement, verb endings, accents and word order.
  • "My answer is right" saves an answer you think should have passed, to share from Settings.
  • Reviews are timed by FSRS, the method behind Anki. Slips and help bring a word back a little sooner.
  • Verb tables for 428 verbs in eleven tenses, opened from Practice or by tapping a verb in any sentence.
  • A grammar book of all 357 rules, sorted by topic and searchable.
  • Dictation practice, and twelve new stories, six of them for beginners. There are now 42.
  • Tapping a word shows the meaning it has in that sentence.
  • Every voice clip was recorded again from the corrected text.
  • Screen readers: buttons, switches and results are labelled and read out. Screens adapt to large text.
  • Fixes from a full review of the French course: wrong options in fourteen grammar questions, capitals on word tiles, punctuation on its own as a choice, and a way round the exam.

0.3.0 22 November 2026

The first public version, with levels A1 and A2.

  • Four separate courses: French, German, European Portuguese and Spanish (Spain). One is active at a time and each keeps its own place.
  • Levels A1 and A2 in every course: 60 units, 300 lessons, 720 words, more than 900 sentences and 180 grammar points.
  • Every word and sentence is spoken, with a slower replay.
  • Fifteen kinds of exercise, in both directions, with tap-a-word meanings and a Why? explanation after each answer.
  • A guide for every unit, a level check for people who already know some of the language, and a word bank with every word learned.
  • Six stories in each course, read aloud line by line, opening through level A2.
  • An exam at the end of A1 and A2: 30 questions from the whole level, 70% to pass. Passing A1 opens A2.
  • Five practice modes: smart review, weak spots, recent mistakes, listening and speaking.
  • Daily goal, streak, one optional daily reminder, and an optional five-lives challenge mode.
  • Up to five people on one phone, and a backup file you save and restore yourself.
  • Light and dark themes and a reduce-motion switch.
  • No internet permission, no account, no adverts.
